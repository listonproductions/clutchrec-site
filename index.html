Support

ClutchRec is a multitrack audio recorder for production sound on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email info@clutchrec.com. Tell us your device, your iOS version and the ClutchRec version (Settings, at the bottom), and what happened.

Your subscription

  • Restore a purchase: in ClutchRec, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Change or cancel: subscriptions are managed by Apple, in your App Store subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
