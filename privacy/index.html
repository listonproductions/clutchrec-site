Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

ClutchRec records audio to your iPhone or iPad. Your recordings, notes, transcripts and reports stay on your device and in the storage you choose (for example iCloud Drive or a folder you pick). We don't receive them.

What leaves your device

  • Your subscription. RevenueCat processes purchases for us. It receives your purchase history and a random identifier, not your name or email.
  • Transcription. When transcription is on, Apple's speech recognition service processes the audio, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Place names. When location is on, Apple Maps turns coordinates into a place name, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Time. ClutchRec asks Apple's and NIST's public time servers for the correct time. No personal data is sent.
  • iCloud. Your settings sync through your own iCloud account.
  • On set. ClutchNet and the on-set report use your local network, only when you turn them on, and nothing is sent to the internet.

We don't sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. There is no account to create.
